Bobobidou privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short

  • Your journal (meals, ingredients, pain episodes) stays on your phone. It is never sent to our servers.
  • If you use food recognition, the photo of your plate is sent to our server and then to OpenAI to work out the ingredients. Our server does not keep it.
  • For this feature you sign in with Google: we keep your name, email address and profile picture.
  • No ads, no analytics, no data sold.

Who we are

Bobobidou is an app developed by Kelly Roussel, as an individual, who is the data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: kellyroussel.development@gmail.com

Your health journal: only on your phone

Meals, ingredients, pain episodes (date, time, intensity) and the analyses built from them are stored in a local database on your device. This health data is never sent to our server or to any third party by the app. Analyses are computed on the phone.

Two cases where this data can leave your phone, at your initiative:

  • CSV export: from the Me tab, you can save a file of your data wherever you choose (Downloads, Drive…). That file is then under your control.
  • Android backup: if your device's automatic backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in the encrypted backup of your Google account. You can turn it off in your phone settings.

Meal photos

Food recognition is optional: you can always type your ingredients yourself.

When you use it, the photo you take or pick is sent, together with the app language, to our server. The server forwards it to the OpenAI API, which returns a list of ingredients. Our server does not keep the photo: it is not written to disk or to a database, and only the ingredient list goes back to the app.

OpenAI acts as a processor. Under its API terms, the data sent is not used to train its models and may be kept for up to 30 days, in particular to detect abuse, before being deleted. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

The number of photos analyzed per day is limited. To enforce this limit, our server counts your photos of the day under a fingerprint (HMAC) of your Google identifier, without the photo or its content. This counter is not linked to your account: it is kept for 7 days, even if you delete your account, so that deleting and recreating an account does not get around the limit. After that, it is erased.

Tip: only photograph your plate, with no recognizable person or document.

Your Google account

Food recognition requires signing in with Google. We only ask for the basic "email" and "profile" permissions. We receive and keep:

  • your email address, name and profile picture URL;
  • Bobobidou's sign-in tokens that keep you signed in. We do not keep Google access tokens.

This information is only used to authenticate you and to protect the recognition service from abuse. It is also kept on your phone to show your account in the Me tab, and erased from it when you log out.

Legal bases

  • Food recognition and account: performing the service you request by using it.
  • Technical logs and photo counter: our legitimate interest in securing the service and limiting abuse.
  • We do not process your health journal: it stays on your device, under your sole control.

Hosting and processors

  • Google Cloud: server hosting (Cloud Run, in Belgium).
  • Supabase: server database (accounts and sign-in tokens).
  • OpenAI: meal photo analysis.
  • Google: sign-in with your Google account.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers outside the European Union rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Deleting your data

  • Journal: erase everything by uninstalling the app, or via Settings › Apps › Bobobidou › Storage › Clear data.
  • Session on the phone: "Log out" button in the Me tab.
  • Account on our server, from the app: Me tab › "Delete my account". Your account and sign-in tokens are deleted immediately and permanently; your journal stays on the phone. Only the photo counter, which holds neither your name nor your email, is kept for up to 7 days.
  • Account on our server, without the app: go to the account deletion page and sign in with the Google account you use in the app. Deletion is immediate, and Bobobidou's access to your Google account is revoked too.
  • Your Google account itself is never deleted. You can remove Bobobidou's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data. To exercise them, email kellyroussel.development@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The recognition service can only be used with a valid sign-in token. Journal data is protected by Android's app sandboxing.

Children

Bobobidou is not intended for children under 15.

Not a medical device

Bobobidou helps you spot leads between your meals and your pain. It is not a diagnosis: talk to a health professional.

Changes

This policy may change with the app. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version; for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
